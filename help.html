What exactly is MarketScope AI?
A trading companion for your phone. Upload a chart screenshot and the AI reads it into a structured trade plan, a mentor-curated desk publishes daily signals and manages them live until they close, and the community is where traders share wins and notes. Everything runs on real market data.
Is any of this financial advice?
No. Analyses and signals are AI-generated or opinion-based technical commentary, and trading always carries risk. Every decision you make is yours — see our Terms of Service for the full picture.
How many free analyses do I get?
Free accounts get a small daily allowance of AI chart analyses. New accounts also start with a free trial of all premium features. If you hit the daily limit on the free tier, you can optionally watch a short rewarded video to unlock one extra analysis for that day.
Why do I see ads?
Ads fund the free tier so it can stay free. They come from Google AdMob, only appear if you consent through Google's official consent form, and are never disguised as signals or community content. Premium subscribers never see ads at all.
Can I turn off personalized ads?
Yes. You can change or withdraw your ad-privacy choice at any time in the app under Settings → Ad privacy, or by resetting your device's advertising ID in Android settings. Declining personalization keeps ads on, but they stop using your advertising identifier. Details are in the Privacy Policy.
What do I get with Premium?
Unrestricted AI analyses, the full signal experience, and a completely ad-free app. Premium renews monthly and you can cancel anytime — access simply continues until the end of the paid period.
How do daily signals work?
Each trading day the desk curates setups — with entry, stop, target and risk-reward levels — then manages the call live: updates, partials and the close, with push alerts as it moves. Closed signals show their outcome so you can follow the track record honestly.
How do I delete my account and data?
In the app: Settings → Delete my account. The request enters a 30-day grace period you can cancel from the app; after that, your account and associated data are permanently erased. You can also email support@marketscopeai.com.
Which payments do you accept?
Subscriptions are billed securely through Paystack and, where available, Google Play. We never see your full card details — only your name, email and subscription status.
