Questions

Asked, answered.

Is MarketScope AI financial advice?
No. Analyses and signals are AI-generated or opinion-based technical commentary. Trading carries real risk and every decision is yours. Full details in our Terms of Service.
How many free analyses do I get?
Free accounts get a daily allowance of AI chart analyses, and new accounts start with a free trial of all premium features. If you hit the daily limit, you can optionally watch a short rewarded video to unlock one extra analysis for that day.
How are the daily signals managed?
Each trading day the desk curates setups with entry, stop, target and R:R — then manages the call live, posting updates, partials and the close, with push alerts as it moves. Closed signals show their real outcome, win or lose.
Why do I see ads on the free tier?
Ads keep the free tier free. They come from Google AdMob, only appear with your consent through Google's official consent form, and are never disguised as signals or community content. Premium subscribers never see ads. See the Privacy Policy for your ad-privacy choices.
What do I get with Premium?
Unrestricted AI analyses and a completely ad-free app. Premium renews monthly and you can cancel anytime — access continues until the end of the paid period.
How do I delete my account?
In the app: Settings → Delete my account. The request has a 30-day grace period you can cancel; after that, your account and data are permanently erased. Details in the Privacy Policy.
Which platforms are supported?
MarketScope AI is a native Android app today — an iOS build is on the roadmap. The website you're on is the home of the desk for everyone.
