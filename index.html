FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is MarketScope AI free?
Yes. The free tier includes 3 chart analyses per day, the latest daily signal, Market View, price alerts, the Learning Hub and full community access. Premium unlocks unlimited analyses, the full signals archive and a more powerful analysis model — with a 7-day free trial.
Where do the daily signals come from?
MarketScope AI generates them daily by studying live market data across forex, gold, indices and crypto. Every signal is published with complete levels and an explanation of the thesis, then managed with live updates until it closes. Outcomes — wins and losses — are scored honestly.
Which platforms is it on?
Android today — a native Kotlin app, not a web wrapper. You can install it right now from the download button on this page. iOS is planned next.
How is my data handled?
Your charts and profile stay in your account and are used to power your analyses. You can request full account deletion anytime from inside the app — support erases it within 30 days, and you can cancel the request yourself before then.
Is this financial advice?
No. MarketScope AI is an analysis and education tool. Trading involves real risk of loss — always do your own research and consult a licensed advisor before trading.
